What this notice covers

Xtan is a mobile craft and maker community experience. People browse project posts, publish image or video work-in-progress, react with likes and favorites, leave comments and stickers, follow makers they enjoy, manage block and report actions, receive inbox style notices, and converse in one-to-one or group style chats that may include text, images, short video, and call-style camera sessions presented inside the app.

Opening Xtan does not depend on a forced membership gate. You may use available community and messaging surfaces under the product rules described in the companion Terms of Use. This notice focuses on privacy-related processing connected to those surfaces and to device permissions you choose to grant.

We describe practices in careful language. No mobile product can guarantee that every technical risk is eliminated; we explain what we intend to do and the limits that still apply.

Signals that may arise while you craft and chat

Depending on how you use Xtan, the following categories may be involved. Not every category is created for every person on every visit.

Craft posts and reactions

  • Titles, captions, and other text you place on a DIY post.
  • Photos or videos you attach as cover media or detail media for a project.
  • Interaction records such as likes, favorites, comments, emoticon tokens, follows, blocks, and reports you initiate.

Messaging and call sessions

  • Message bodies, media attachments, and recall or view actions within chat threads.
  • Group conversation labels and membership lists that appear in your chat list.
  • Transient camera preview and audio capture during video recording or call-style screens, while those features are active.

Inbox style notices

  • Notification text about community events such as comments, follows, or stranger leave-message style alerts when those surfaces are enabled in the product.

On-device gesture checks

  • When a face-nod style liveness or attentiveness check is presented, the front camera stream may be analyzed on the device with computer-vision techniques to detect a nod gesture. The purpose is a short safety or attentiveness confirmation related to community use. Face video from that check is intended to be processed locally for the gesture result and is not designed as a lasting biometric archive uploaded by default.

Technical and preference residue

  • Local preference flags, seed or cache version markers, draft media file names, and similar app state kept in local storage on your device.
  • Device permission status for camera, microphone, and photo library as reported by the operating system.
  • Crash or diagnostic fragments that the operating system or development tooling may surface when the app fails, which can include device model and OS version.

Device capabilities you may allow

Xtan requests system permissions only when a feature needs them. You can deny a permission; related features may then be unavailable until you change the setting in system controls.

  • Camera. Used to capture craft photos, record short project videos, run face-nod style on-device checks when shown, and present call-style camera previews.
  • Microphone. Used when recording video with sound or during call-style sessions that include voice.
  • Photo library read access. Used when you pick existing images or videos for posts or chat attachments.
  • Photo library write access. Used when you choose to save an image or video from the app into your library.

Permission prompts and system settings remain under your control. Xtan does not need continuous background camera or microphone access for ordinary feed browsing.

Why those signals are processed

We process the categories above for purposes that match the craft community product:

  • Displaying feeds, post detail pages, comments, and maker interaction states.
  • Delivering chat threads, media messages, and call-style previews you start.
  • Showing inbox notices tied to community activity.
  • Supporting safety tools such as block and report flows and short on-device gesture checks when presented.
  • Remembering local preferences and cached media so the app can reopen in a coherent state.
  • Improving reliability by reviewing crash or error patterns when they are available to the operator.
  • Responding to inquiries you send to marigold.riftweave@loomkiln.app.

We do not treat browsing the DIY feed as a license to sell your personal information as a standalone commodity. Processing is oriented to operating and safeguarding the Xtan experience.

Where information may sit on your device or travel

Local persistence

Much of the demo and early-product behavior of Xtan stores community content, chat history, preference flags, and media file references in the application sandbox on your device (for example Documents-style storage and secure key storage facilities provided by the OS). Clearing app data or removing the app typically removes that local residue, subject to how your device and OS manage backups.

Network paths

When network features are enabled in a given build, posts, messages, media, and interaction events may be transmitted to servers that host community content so other participants can see what you publish. Transmission uses industry-common transport protections where the platform and hosting stack support them. Absolute immunity from interception or misuse cannot be promised for any networked application.

On-device vision for face-nod checks

Face-nod analysis is designed to evaluate a live gesture on the device. The resulting pass or fail state may be retained as a local product flag. Continuous face video from that check is not intended to be uploaded as a default cloud biometric library.

Helpers outside the Xtan binary

Xtan relies on Apple platform frameworks for camera capture, photo library access, microphone input, on-device vision analysis, and secure storage. Those frameworks operate under Apple’s terms and system privacy controls.

If a future release integrates hosting, content delivery, crash reporting, or similar infrastructure vendors, those vendors would process only what is needed to provide their contracted function and would be expected to apply reasonable confidentiality and security commitments. This notice will be updated when such integrations materially change what leaves your device.

We may disclose information when required by applicable laws, lawful process, or to protect the safety of people using Xtan or the integrity of the service, using competent dispute resolution bodies or authorities as relevant.

Practical safeguards we apply

  • Limiting permission prompts to features that need them.
  • Preferring on-device processing for face-nod style gesture checks.
  • Using OS-provided secure storage facilities for sensitive local tokens or flags when the product requires them.
  • Access controls and review practices on operator systems that handle support mail or hosted content, where such systems exist.

These measures reduce risk; they do not create an absolute shield against every form of unauthorized access, device compromise, or third-party failure.

How long material may remain available

Local drafts, caches, and preference flags generally remain until you clear app data, remove the app, or the product overwrites them during normal operation.

Published craft posts, comments, chat messages, and related media remain available while they are part of the active community dataset for that installation or hosted environment, unless you delete them through in-product controls where offered, or the operator removes them for safety, legal, or operational reasons.

Report and block records may be kept for a period reasonably needed to evaluate misuse and protect other makers. Retention periods may vary with applicable laws and the nature of the content.

Choices you can exercise

  • Revoke camera, microphone, or photo library access in system Settings; related Xtan features will stop working until restored.
  • Avoid attaching media or limit what you write in posts and chats.
  • Use block and report tools when another participant’s content or contact is unwanted.
  • Remove locally stored app data by deleting the app or clearing storage through device tools.
  • Email marigold.riftweave@loomkiln.app to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information that the operator can reasonably locate and control.

Because Xtan does not impose a mandatory membership gate to open the app, many preferences live on the device itself. Responses to privacy requests will reflect what the operator can actually retrieve for your device or correspondence.

Younger audiences

Xtan is intended for adults who participate in a maker community. The product is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Xtan, contact marigold.riftweave@loomkiln.app so appropriate steps can be considered, including deletion where feasible.

Where applicable laws set a higher age of digital consent, we expect guardians and older teens to follow those rules when using craft sharing and messaging features.

When this notice changes

Craft products evolve. When we change how Xtan handles information in a meaningful way, we will revise this HTML notice and make the updated version available in or alongside the app. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised text.

The version you are reading became operative on September 8, 2026. Earlier copies no longer describe current practice once replaced.
